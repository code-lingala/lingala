kotambola

to walkmarcher

Imperative (singular): tambola · plural: bo-tambola

EN To walk, to travel on foot. Kotambola covers walking, traveling, and managing — kotambola malamu = it is going well.FR Marcher, voyager à pied. Kotambola couvre la marche, le voyage, et la gestion — kotambola malamu = ça se passe bien.LN Kotambola — kotambola na makolo. Kotambola ezipi kotambola, mobembo, mpe bobongisi — kotambola malamu = ezali kotambola malamu.
