kotinda

to sendenvoyer

Imperative (singular): tinda · plural: bo-tinda

EN To send (corrected example: mokanda = letter, not nkanda = anger). Kotinda is sending — letters, money, messages, news.FR Envoyer (exemple corrigé : mokanda = lettre, pas nkanda = colère). Kotinda, c'est envoyer — lettres, argent, messages, nouvelles.LN Kotinda — kotinda eloko to nsango. Kotinda elimboli kotinda — mikanda, mbongo, nsango, nsango ya sika.
