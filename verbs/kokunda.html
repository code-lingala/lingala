Verbs · buryenterrer

kokunda

buryenterrer

Imperative (singular): kunda · plural: bo-kunda

EN To bury — laying the body in the earth. Kokunda is to bury. Death gathers the whole family; to kokunda an ebembe is a slow, sung ritual — the closing of one life and the gathering of those it touched.FR Enterrer — poser le corps dans la terre. Kokunda, c'est enterrer. La mort rassemble toute la famille ; kokunda un ebembe est un rituel lent, chanté — la fermeture d'une vie et le rassemblement de ceux qu'elle a touchés.LN Kokunda — kotia nzoto na mabele. Kokunda elimboli kokunda. Liwa esangisi libota mobimba; kokunda ebembe ezali eyenga ya malembe, ya boyembi — kokanga bomoyi moko mpe kosangisa bato oyo bayebanaki na yango.
