kobikisa

to cure / healguérir / soigner

Imperative (singular): bikisa · plural: bo-bikisa

EN To heal — bringing the body back to itself. Kobikisa (rooted in bomoi, life) is to heal, cure, or save. The clinic and the church share this verb; "nkisi oyo ekobikisa yo" — this medicine will heal you — is the steady reassurance of care. (Kobowa is an older, less standard form.)FR Guérir — ramener le corps à lui-même. Kobikisa (de bomoi, la vie) signifie guérir, soigner, sauver. La clinique et l'église partagent ce verbe ; « nkisi oyo ekobikisa yo » — ce médicament te guérira — est la promesse régulière du soin. (Kobowa est une forme plus ancienne, moins standard.)LN Kobikisa — kozongisa nzoto na esika na yango. Kobikisa (oyo euti na bomoyi) elimboli kobikisa, kosalisa, mpe kobikisa moto. Lopitalo na ndako ya Nzambe basangani na liloba moko; « nkisi oyo ekobikisa yo » — nkisi oyo ekobikisa yo — ezali maloba ya kondima oyo ekitisi motema. (Kobowa ezali ndenge ya kala mpe oyo basaleli mingi te.)
