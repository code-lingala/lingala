Verbs · hurtse blesser

kozoka

hurtse blesser

Imperative (singular): zoka · plural: bo-zoka

EN To get hurt — the body taking the day's mishap. Kozoka is to be wounded or hurt. The child who azoki in the yard, the worker on a sharp tool — to kozoka is the small or large damage life can deal.FR Se blesser — le corps prenant l'accident du jour. Kozoka, c'est être blessé. L'enfant qui azoki dans la cour, l'ouvrier sur un outil tranchant — kozoka est le dommage, petit ou grand, que la vie peut infliger.LN Kozoka — nzoto ezwi mpasi ya mokolo. Kozoka elimboli kozoka to kozwa mpasi. Mwana oyo azoki na lopango, mosali na esaleli ya makasi — kozoka ezali libebi, ya mokemoke to ya monene, oyo bomoyi ekoki kopesa.
