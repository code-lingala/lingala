Verbs · to swellenfler / gonfler

kovimba

to swellenfler / gonfler

Imperative (singular): vimba · plural: bo-vimba

EN To swell. Kovimba names the body swelling — bites, sprains, infections. A monganga is consulted when it persists.FR Enfler. Kovimba nomme le corps qui gonfle — piqûres, foulures, infections. On consulte un monganga quand cela persiste.LN Kovimba — kovimba ya nzoto. Kovimba etangaka nzoto oyo evimbi — mwingi, mokuwa oyo etyemi, makono. Batunaka monganga ntango eumeli.
