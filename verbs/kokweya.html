kokweya

falltomber

Imperative (singular): kweya · plural: bo-kweya

EN To fall — the body going down despite itself. Kokweya is to fall. The child who kwei, the leaf from the branch, the price in the market — the verb covers every kind of going down.FR Tomber — le corps qui descend malgré lui. Kokweya, c'est tomber. L'enfant qui kwei, la feuille de la branche, le prix au marché — le verbe couvre toute forme de descente.LN Kokweya — nzoto oyo ezali kokita ata bolingo na yango te. Kokweya elimboli kokweya. Mwana oyo akwei, lokasa euti na etape, motuya na zando — liloba ezipi ndenge nyonso ya kokita.
