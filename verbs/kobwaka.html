kobwaka

to throw away / discardjeter / se debarrasser de

Imperative (singular): bwaka · plural: bo-bwaka

EN Kobwaka — to throw away, discard. Used for trash, but also for abandoning something dear (kobwaka libala = to throw away the marriage). A weighted verb.FR Kobwaka — jeter, abandonner. Utilisé pour les ordures, mais aussi pour abandonner quelque chose de cher (kobwaka libala = jeter le mariage). Un verbe lourd de sens.LN Kobwaka — kobwaka, kotika. Basaleli yango mpo na bosoto, kasi mpe mpo na kotika eloko ya motuya (kobwaka libala = kobwaka libala). Liloba ya kilo.
