koyebisa

informinformer / faire savoir

Imperative (singular): yebisa · plural: bo-yebisa

EN To inform — passing the news along. Koyebisa is to inform or tell. From a quiet whisper to a public announcement, koyebisa carries what one person knows into the room of many.FR Informer — faire passer la nouvelle. Koyebisa, c'est informer ou dire. Du chuchotement tranquille à l'annonce publique, koyebisa porte ce qu'une personne sait dans la pièce des autres.LN Koyebisa — koleka nsango. Koyebisa elimboli koyebisa to koyebisa. Banda kotanga ya nse kino kosakola ya polele, koyebisa ememi oyo moto moko ayebi na ndako ya bato mingi.
