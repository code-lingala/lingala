kotuna

to askdemander / questionner

Imperative (singular): tuna · plural: bo-tuna

EN To ask a question. Kotuna is to ask for information (vs. kosenga = to ask for a thing/favor).FR Poser une question. Kotuna, c'est demander une information (vs. kosenga = demander une chose ou une faveur).LN Kotuna — kotuna motuna. Kotuna elimboli kotuna mpo na koyeba (vs. kosenga = kosenga eloko to ngolu).
