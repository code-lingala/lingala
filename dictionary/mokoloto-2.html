mokoloto

mo-ko-LO-to

line / queue

Generalnoun

French: file / ligne

Bato bazali kotelema na mokoloto.

People are standing in line.

Les gens sont debout en file.

Line, queue, row (variant; see #456). Mokoloto: the waiting line for bread, bank, or bureau.
