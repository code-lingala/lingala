kozala na bomoi

ko-ZA-la NA bo-MO-i

to live

Generalnoun

French: vivre

Tozali na bomoi mpo na Nzambe.

We live for God.

Nous vivons pour Dieu.

To live, to be alive. Kozala = to be, bomoi = life. Kozala na bomoi = literally to be with life. A weighty phrase.
